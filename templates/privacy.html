Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 26, 2026

1. Data We Collect

To provide our services, we collect the following information:

  • Account Information: Username and email address provided during registration.
  • Financial Data: Expense amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions that you manually enter.
  • Technical Data: Basic browser information and IP addresses for security and performance monitoring.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the collected data exclusively to:

  • Provide and maintain the expense tracking functionality.
  • Generate spending insights and category breakdowns.
  • Ensure the security of your account and prevent fraudulent activity.
  • Improve the user experience and application performance.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely in our databases. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction. Your password is stored using a secure hashing algorithm.

4. Third Party Services

Expenzy does not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal financial data to third parties. We may use limited third-party services for infrastructure (e.g., cloud hosting), but these providers are bound by strict confidentiality agreements.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact us at privacy@expenzy.com.
